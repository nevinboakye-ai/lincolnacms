Become a member

Membership is open to students of every course and background at the University of Lincoln — £10 for the year.

What you get

  • A mentor who gets it

    Priority access to the Sankofa Mentorship scheme.

  • First access to events

    Free or discounted entry to socials, workshops and the Midlands Medics Gala.

  • Opportunities, first look

    Work experience, bursaries and volunteering shared with members before anyone else.

  • A community

    People who understand your journey — on the course and off it.
