Opportunities

Work experience, bursaries & beyond

We surface opportunities from our partners and the wider community — clinical shadowing, scholarships aimed at Black and mixed-heritage students, and volunteering roles.

Work experience

[Placeholder] Clinical Shadowing — Lincoln County Hospital

A one-week shadowing placement for pre-clinical students, arranged in partnership with local NHS trusts.

Express interest

[Placeholder] A one-week placement shadowing doctors across two or three departments — a good early taste of clinical practice for pre-clinical students. Spaces are limited and offered on a first-come basis.

Scholarship

[Placeholder] Diversity in Medicine Bursary

A bursary aimed at Black and mixed-heritage students in medicine and healthcare degrees, covering course costs.

Learn more

[Placeholder] Covers a portion of course-related costs for eligible students — details on amount, eligibility and how to apply to follow once confirmed with the funding partner.

Volunteering

[Placeholder] Community Health Champions

Volunteer with a local charity delivering health education workshops to underserved communities in Lincoln.

Express interest

[Placeholder] No clinical experience required — training is provided before your first session. A great way to build community-facing skills alongside your degree.

Outreach

[Placeholder] Widening Access Mentor

Support LACMS's schools outreach programme by mentoring a sixth-form student applying to medicine.

Find out more

[Placeholder] A one-to-one commitment across the academic year, supporting a local sixth-form student through their UCAS application and interview prep. Full guidance provided.

Careers

[Placeholder] NHS Summer Internship Programme

A paid summer internship for penultimate-year students, open through an external NHS partner.

Learn more

[Placeholder] A paid, six-week summer placement with an NHS partner trust — application window and eligibility criteria to be confirmed closer to the time.
