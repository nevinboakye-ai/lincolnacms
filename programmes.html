Programmes

Structured support, all year round

Beyond socials, LACMS runs ongoing programmes to support members academically, professionally and personally.

Flagship programme

Sankofa Mentorship

Our intergenerational mentorship scheme organises members into small "Sankofa Circles" grouped by shared interest - each spanning a sixth-form aspiring medic, a medical student from every year of study, an F1/F2 alumni doctor, and a senior doctor or consultant.

Everyone supports the person just behind them, creating one continuous chain of mentorship from sixth form through medical school and into clinical practice. Circles meet termly, with additional check-ins between individual mentors and mentees.
