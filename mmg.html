Flagship Event · 21 November 2026

Midlands Medics Gala

A black-tie night of awards, dinner and dancing — in collaboration with 7 universities from across the Midlands.

Why the gala exists

The Midlands Medics Gala brings together African and Caribbean heritage healthcare students from eight universities across the region for one night — a celebration that no single society could put on alone.

Students on medicine, pharmacy and allied healthcare courses are often one of a handful of Black or Caribbean students on their course, sometimes in their entire year group. The gala exists to turn that isolation into a network — friendships, mentors and contacts that reach well beyond Lincoln, built while celebrating a year of achievement in black-tie style.

It's also a chance to recognise the people who've made the year what it is, through awards voted on by attendees themselves, and to strengthen the partnership between the committees who put it together — a genuinely shared production, not one university hosting the rest.

  • A pan-Midlands network

    Meet students from Nottingham, Birmingham, Leicester and more, doing the same courses in the same region.

  • A night of recognition

    Awards voted on by attendees — a proper celebration of the year, not just a formal dinner.

  • Built together

    Planned jointly by all 8 university committees — genuinely shared, not hosted by one.
