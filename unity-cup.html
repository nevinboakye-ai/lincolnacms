Raffle rules
  • You must donate at least the minimum shown to the LACMS JustGiving page to enter.
  • One entry per person (one email address). Entrants should be 18 or over.
  • The committee checks every entry against the JustGiving donations, and only checked entries go into the draw.
  • The winner is picked at random during the draw. The committee’s decision on eligibility is final.
  • The winner is contacted by email and the prize is a gift card, with no cash alternative.
  • We delete raffle names and emails after the event. See the privacy notice.
