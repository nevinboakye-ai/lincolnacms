Cookies & storage

What this site stores on your device, and your choices.

Last updated 30 October 2026

This page explains what this website stores on your device and lets you choose. We store things in your browser’s storage (“local storage” and “session storage”) rather than classic cookies; the rules (PECR) treat them the same, so we call them all cookies here.

Your choices

Essential

Keeps you signed in, remembers light or dark mode and the choices on this page. The site can’t work without them, so they are always on.

Always on
Analytics (visit counting)

Stores a random ID on your device so we can count visits and see which pages are used. It never identifies you. Off unless you turn it on.

You can change your mind at any time from here or from “Cookie settings” in the footer of any page. We ask again after 12 months.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising, remarketing or social-media tracking.
  • No Google Analytics or similar third-party analytics. Our own visit counter stores its data in our database only.
  • No third-party fonts or scripts – they are hosted with the site, so your browser doesn’t contact other companies when you load a page.
  • No embedded videos or social buttons. Links to Instagram, LinkedIn and TikTok are ordinary links.

If your browser sends a “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control signal, we treat analytics as off.

You can also clear stored items at any time in your browser settings. For how we use personal information in general, see the privacy notice.
