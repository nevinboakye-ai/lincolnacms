The short version
  • We collect only what we need to run the society: who you are, your course and year, and what you do on this site.
  • We never sell your data, and we don’t use advertising or social-media tracking.
  • Analytics (counting visits) only happens if you say yes in the cookie banner.
  • You can download your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask us to stop using it – see your rights.
  • Questions or worries? Email acms@lincolnsu.com.

1. Who we are and who is responsible

This website is run by the committee of the Lincoln African Caribbean Medical Society (LACMS), a student society of the University of Lincoln Students’ Union (ULSU). We are not the University of Lincoln.

LACMS decides how this website uses your personal information, and is responsible for looking after it under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018 and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), as amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025. LACMS is affiliated to ULSU, so ULSU’s own data protection policies are a useful reference for us, and its Data Protection Officer’s contact details are below if you would rather raise something with the Students’ Union directly.

Membership itself is bought from and recorded by ULSU, not by us. ULSU looks after that membership record under its own privacy notice. This notice covers what this website holds.

  • LACMS (this website): acms@lincolnsu.com
  • ULSU’s Data Protection Officer (for anything involving the Students’ Union): compliance@lincolnsu.com
  • ULSU’s complaints team (if you prefer to go to the Students’ Union): complaints@lincolnsu.com
  • Address: Lincoln Students’ Union, Brayford Pool, Lincoln, LN6 7TS

3. Sensitive information

Some information deserves extra care. We keep it to the minimum, make it optional, and tell you plainly:

  • Heritage (ethnic origin). LACMS is for people of African and Caribbean heritage and open to everyone. We do not ask about ethnicity to join. The Sankofa application has one optional question about your country of heritage, used only to help the committee match Circles. We only use it if you answer, and your answer is treated as special category data on the basis of your explicit consent. Leave it blank and nothing changes.
  • Wellbeing. The optional Sankofa weekly check-in asks how you are feeling. It is shared with your Circle’s mentors and the Sankofa committee so you can be supported. It is optional, it is not a counselling or crisis service, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.
  • Student number. Used only to check your membership against ULSU’s list. It is visible only to the committee members who approve accounts.
  • Messages and safeguarding. If someone is at risk, we may share the minimum necessary with the people who can help, such as the Students’ Union or the appropriate authorities (see Safeguarding).

4. Who can see your information

  • The committee. Committee members who have been given access, only for what their role needs. Sankofa managers can see Circle information. An administrator can preview the site as a member to check it works; this is read-only, recorded, and never shows private messages.
  • Other members. The Network (when the committee has switched it on) shows your name, course and year, and any LinkedIn link or bio you choose to add, to people the committee has given Network access to, which can include approved healthcare professionals as well as members. If you would rather not appear, tell us and we will hide you; comments and board posts show your name; your Circle sees your name, role and any intro you write. Messages are visible only to the people in the conversation, and, if someone reports a message, to the committee members who review the report.
  • The public. Only what we deliberately publish: the committee page, Member of the Month winners, events and gallery.
  • Partners. Event partners can be given a private link to anonymised event results (ratings, comments and counts). Names and email addresses are never shown on it.
  • The Students’ Union. We don’t routinely share your information with ULSU. If it ever asked for something (for example to check membership, or for safeguarding reasons), we would only share what is necessary and lawful.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Be informed – this notice.
  • Access your information. Signed-in members can download it instantly from My data & privacy.
  • Correct anything wrong. You can edit your profile yourself, or ask us.
  • Erasure – ask us to delete your information.
  • Restrict or object to how we use it.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on it (for example analytics, the Network, optional answers).
  • Data portability – receive what you gave us in a machine-readable file (the download above).
  • Not be subject to solely automated decisions with significant effects. We don’t make any. Sankofa match suggestions are reviewed by people.

How to use them: use the data request form, email acms@lincolnsu.com, or contact ULSU’s Data Protection Officer at compliance@lincolnsu.com. It’s free. We will reply within one month (we’ll tell you if we need up to two more months for a complex request, or need to confirm who you are first). Some things we can’t always delete – for example a safeguarding record, or something we are legally required to keep – and we will explain why.

7. Emails

Service emails (account approval, payment, security, safeguarding and anything needed to run your membership) are not marketing and everyone receives them. Society updates (news, events, opportunities, programmes) are sent to members on the basis of legitimate interests, always say who they are from, and you can opt out in one click in My data & privacy, or by emailing us. We never send promotional emails for other organisations.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

We only store essential items on your device (keeping you signed in, your light/dark choice, reminders you have dismissed). Anything else, such as visit counting, needs your consent. Details and your choices are on the cookies page.

9. Keeping it safe

  • The website is served over HTTPS, so your connection is encrypted.
  • Passwords are stored scrambled (hashed) by our login provider; we cannot read them.
  • Every table in our database has row-level security switched on, so people can only read what they are allowed to.
  • Administrator access is limited to named committee members, and sensitive actions are recorded.
  • Files and messages are stored securely and encrypted at rest.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach could put you at risk we will tell you and the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) as the law requires, and where appropriate we will let the Students’ Union know.

10. Complaints

If you are unhappy about how we have used your information, please tell us first at acms@lincolnsu.com (you can also go to ULSU’s complaints team at complaints@lincolnsu.com) and we will acknowledge it within 30 days and reply without undue delay. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113. You don’t have to complain to us first to complain to the ICO.

11. Changes to this notice

If we change this notice in a way that matters, members will be asked to read it again the next time they sign in. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
