Safeguarding & wellbeing

How we look after members, how to raise a concern, and where to get help.

On this page
  1. Our approach
  2. Raising a concern
  3. Mentoring and wellbeing

Last updated 30 October 2026

If someone is in immediate danger, call 999.

For urgent help with your own mental health: University of Lincoln Student Wellbeing on 01522 886400, NHS 111 (option 2), or Samaritans on 116 123 (free, 24 hours). You can text SHOUT to 85258.

Our approach

LACMS is a society of the University of Lincoln Students’ Union (ULSU) and follows the ULSU Group Safeguarding of Children and Vulnerable Adults Policy. We pass any safeguarding concern straight to ULSU’s Safeguarding Officers (the ULSU Group Chief Executive and Deputy Chief Executive), as ULSU’s policy asks of its societies. We don’t investigate concerns ourselves, and we don’t promise to keep safeguarding information confidential, because the person’s safety comes first.

Raising a concern

  • About a message or someone’s behaviour on the site: use “Report” in the chat, or tell us here. You can also email acms@lincolnsu.com with “Safeguarding” in the subject.
  • Directly to the Students’ Union: help@lincolnsu.com, or ULSU reception on 01522 886006. Complaints: complaints@lincolnsu.com.
  • University of Lincoln Student Wellbeing: 01522 886400.

When you tell us something worrying, we will listen, tell you who we have to pass it to, and not ask you for more detail than is needed. Reports go to the society’s committee, who pass them to ULSU. If we are concerned someone may be at risk of radicalisation we will also pass it to ULSU, as ULSU is required to by the Prevent duty.

Mentoring and wellbeing

  • Mentors are volunteers, and must keep to professional boundaries and use the platform for contact.
  • The optional Sankofa weekly check-in lets you say you’d like someone to check in with you. It is not a crisis service and it isn’t monitored around the clock. If you need help now, use the numbers above.
  • You can ask us at any time to move you to another Circle, pause your participation, or delete your check-in data.
