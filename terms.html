Terms of use

The rules for using this website and the members hub.

Last updated 30 September 2026

These terms apply when you use the LACMS website and members hub. LACMS is a student society of the University of Lincoln Students’ Union (ULSU). By using the site you agree to them. If you don’t agree, please don’t use the members areas.

1. Who can use it

  • The public pages are open to everyone.
  • The members hub is for current LACMS members (who joined through ULSU), approved healthcare professionals, and guests the committee invites.
  • Keep your login to yourself. Tell us straight away if you think someone else has used your account.
  • Membership is bought from ULSU. We can pause or close an account that isn’t a genuine, current membership.

2. How we expect everyone to behave

LACMS is a community, and our members are future and current clinicians. These rules are in the same spirit as ULSU’s Code of Conduct, Equal Opportunities Policy and Student Members’ Social Media Policy, which also apply to members of ULSU societies. On this site, you must not:

  • harass, bully, threaten, discriminate against or abuse anyone, or share content that is explicit, hateful or illegal;
  • impersonate anyone, or post fake or manipulated content (including AI-generated content intended to mislead);
  • share another person’s private information without their permission;
  • share any patient information or anything that could identify a patient, including in comments, messages, mentoring or resources. This is a professional duty, not just a site rule;
  • post advice that is presented as medical advice, or claim to speak for LACMS, ULSU or the University without permission;
  • upload malware, try to access other people’s accounts or data, or misuse the site;
  • use the site for advertising or to promote anything without the committee’s agreement.

Comments and messages are for friendly, respectful discussion. Free speech and robust debate are welcome; harassment is not.

3. What you post

  • You own what you post, but you give LACMS a free licence to show it on the site for the purpose you posted it (for example, a resource you upload is shared with members).
  • Only upload things you have the right to share. Don’t upload copyrighted material you don’t have permission for. If you think something on the site infringes your rights, email acms@lincolnsu.com with a link and we will review it promptly and remove it where appropriate.
  • Learning resources are shared in good faith and are not a substitute for official University materials or clinical guidance.

4. Reporting and moderation

You can report a message from inside the chat, or use the report a concern form or email us. The committee reviews reports, and may remove content, suspend messaging or close accounts that break these terms. Serious concerns about safety, abuse or illegal content may be passed to ULSU or, where needed, the police (see Safeguarding). We keep a record of reports and the action taken. Content that is reported may be kept as evidence.

5. Sankofa mentoring

  • Mentors are volunteers sharing their experience. They are not your clinical supervisors, and mentoring is not medical, legal or career guarantees.
  • Keep contact with mentors and mentees on the platform and within professional boundaries.
  • If something makes you uncomfortable, report it (see section 4).

6. Availability and liability

We run the site voluntarily and try to keep it available and accurate, but we can’t promise it will always be error-free or uninterrupted. Nothing in these terms limits any right you have that can’t be limited by law, including liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud. Otherwise, to the extent the law allows, LACMS and its volunteer committee are not liable for loss arising from using the site. External links (including to ULSU and social media) are provided for convenience and we don’t control them.

7. Other things

  • How we use your personal information is explained in the privacy notice.
  • We may update these terms. If the changes matter, we will tell members when they next sign in.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of England and Wales.
  • Questions or complaints: acms@lincolnsu.com. You can also use ULSU’s complaints procedure (complaints@lincolnsu.com).
